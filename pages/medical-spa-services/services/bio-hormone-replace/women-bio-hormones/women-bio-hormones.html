Bio-identical Hormone Replacement

women-bio-hormone

Hormones are the signals our bodies send to our organs. We get older, we make less hormones. With less signals, our organs slow along with our bodies.

Women experience a more complex hormonal transition because their ovaries gradually stop producing several hormones,

  • Estrogen (Estradiol)
  • Progesterone
  • DHEA
  • Growth Hormone
  • Melatonin
  • Cortisol
  • Testosterone

From around age 35, hormone production begins to change subtly. By the time a woman reaches perimenopause (typically 40s), hormone levels fluctuate dramatically before eventually settling into menopause.
