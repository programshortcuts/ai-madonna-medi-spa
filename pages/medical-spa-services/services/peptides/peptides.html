Bioactive peptides are natural or lab-created compounds made up of short chains of 3–30 amino acids. They play important roles in the body, supporting functions like cellular protection, temperature regulation, and immune response.

Natural Functions in the Body:

  • Hormone signaling: Regulate blood sugar (like insulin) and control appetite.
  • Pain relief: Block pain signals and manage stress (like endorphins).
  • Cellular communication: Tell cells how to move, grow, and heal.
