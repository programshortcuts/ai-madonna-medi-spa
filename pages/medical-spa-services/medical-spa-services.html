We offer face and neck infrared treatment that helps reduce wrinkles while stimulating collagen. Infrared therapy has also been shown to reduce inflammation, improve circulation, and promote healing.

Infrared light therapy uses specific wavelengths of light to penetrate the skin and stimulate cellular function. This non-invasive treatment promotes collagen production, reduces inflammation, and enhances circulation, leading to improved skin tone, texture, and overall skin health. It can also aid in pain relief and muscle recovery.

Depending on the wavelength used, infrared light triggers distinct responses in the body:

  • Near-Infrared (NIR): These shorter, low-energy wavelengths stimulate the mitochondria (the cell’s powerhouse) to produce more energy. This process speeds up skin renewal, tissue growth, and muscle recovery after workouts.
  • Mid and Far-Infrared (MIR/FIR): These longer wavelengths generate deep heat, raising your core temperature. This deep penetration widens blood vessels (vasodilation) to relieve musculoskeletal pain and induce sweating, which helps the body detoxify.
